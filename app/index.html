01

Map the source

SAFER records are joined by water-system ID, with location and service-area coverage reported.

02

Allocate the budget

Priority = people served × status weight × (1 + equity priority × equity index) × (1 + small-system priority for small systems). The optimizer maximizes this weighted score within the rounded-cost budget. It does not necessarily maximize raw population or predict health outcomes. The comparison rule funds failing systems first, then largest population, using the same rounded costs; it is not the state’s actual process.

03

Challenge the costs

Baseline costs use the state’s 2024 per-connection averages, scaled to the 2024 statewide total on a 2026 system list. Reported median county error is about 41%; system-level accuracy is unverified. Cost scenarios hold your priorities fixed and assume independent lognormal errors with a 90% range of 0.5×–2×. Selection frequency describes these assumptions, not real-world success probability.

Planning limits

Population served is potential reach, not confirmed unique beneficiaries or measured health improvement. Readiness, project alternatives, funding already committed, ongoing operations, and consolidation feasibility are not modeled. Missing equity inputs are shown in each system’s details. Historical funding is a descriptive comparison, not outcome validation. Cost-bin boundaries can create abrupt changes in estimated cost.
