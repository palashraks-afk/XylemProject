Why check funding first?

A water system brings drinking water to homes, schools, or businesses. It can have more than one project to fix or improve it.

  1. Make a plan

    EquiFlow looks at costs, how many people a system serves, and how much help it needs.

  2. Check what already has money

    A funding record tells you what work has money approved.

  3. See who else could get help

    Leave a water system out of the example and let EquiFlow pick again with the same budget.

Systems with construction funding can be left out of this practice plan. Real funding decisions happen separately.

    How the model works

    This example uses an equity weight of 1, a five-year estimate for systems at risk, and costs without route adjustments. A water system can have other needs even when one of its projects already has money.
