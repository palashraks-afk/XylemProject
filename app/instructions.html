How to use EquiFlow

See what a budget could cover for California drinking water systems, then check the evidence for individual projects.

Start with the $500M example →

  1. Choose a budget

    In the Funding planner, pick a budget or move the slider. The plan updates as you go. Open Funding priorities to set aside part of the budget for small systems.

  2. See what changes

    Your funding plan shows how many systems the model selects, how many people they serve, and what the plan might cost. Choose Pin for comparison, then change the budget to see the difference.

  3. Find a water system

    Search by name or ID, or select a dot on the map. The system details show whether it was selected and its estimated cost. Try Find East Orosi for an example.

  4. Check project funding

    From a system’s details, choose Check project funding evidence. In the funding review, select Try East Orosi to see how setting aside its documented project changes the list.

What does “selected” mean?

EquiFlow suggests systems to review based on your settings. Costs and populations are estimates, and only some systems have funding records in the app. Check the linked sources before acting on a plan.
