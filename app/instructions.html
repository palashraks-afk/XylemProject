How to use EquiFlow

EquiFlow helps you make a practice plan for California drinking water funding. Follow the walkthrough and try the real controls.

Start walkthrough →

  1. Choose a budget

    Pick an amount or move the slider in the Funding planner. The numbers update on their own.

  2. See what changes

    Click Compare plans, then try a different budget. See how many more or fewer people the plan could reach.

  3. Find a water system

    Search by name or click a dot. Try See East Orosi on the map to see what a system’s details look like.

  4. Check project funding

    Choose Check for existing funding in a system’s details. Read what the money covers. The review lets you make a new example plan with that system left out and the same budget.

    Open the East Orosi example →

What does “selected” mean?

“Selected” means the model put the system in the plan. The plan is an estimate. Funding records show which work has money approved.
